# Platform Foundation

Platform Foundation provides shared operational services:

- Jobs for queued and retryable work.
- Events for lifecycle and integration signals.
- Audit for immutable decision history.
- Notifications for user and system attention.
- Activity for human-readable recent work.
- Health for dependency and subsystem status.
- Policies for allowed actions and quality gates.

Foundation services do not make planning, repository, execution, validation, or QA decisions.
