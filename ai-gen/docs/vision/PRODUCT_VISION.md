# 1. Executive Summary

HEI (Hubbell Engineering Intelligence) is an Engineering Intelligence Platform designed to transform project knowledge into executable engineering context. It bridges the gap between project planning, software development, quality engineering, and AI-assisted implementation.

Unlike traditional AI assistants that primarily generate code, HEI understands the engineering lifecycle. It captures project knowledge, structures engineering intent, and delivers contextual guidance throughout planning, execution, testing, and validation.

HEI is built on the AI Gen Platform, allowing the same architecture to power future enterprise products across different organizations and domains.

---

# 2. The Problem

Modern software engineering teams face several recurring challenges:

* Project knowledge is scattered across Azure DevOps, repositories, documentation, and conversations.
* AI coding assistants lack deep understanding of project architecture and business context.
* Developers repeatedly provide the same context when interacting with AI tools.
* Planning artifacts often become disconnected from implementation.
* Acceptance criteria are generic and difficult to validate.
* QA activities begin too late in the development lifecycle.
* Repository knowledge is underutilized and rarely reused.
* Engineering knowledge is lost as teams evolve.

These challenges reduce delivery quality, increase onboarding time, and create inconsistency across software projects.

---

# 3. Vision

To become the Engineering Intelligence Platform that transforms project knowledge into high-quality software delivery by combining planning, execution, validation, and AI orchestration.

---

# 4. Mission

Enable engineering teams to build software with greater confidence by providing structured project intelligence, reusable engineering knowledge, and guided workflows throughout the software development lifecycle.

---

# 5. Product Philosophy

HEI is founded on the following principles:

* Project knowledge is more valuable than isolated prompts.
* AI should augment engineers, not replace engineering judgment.
* Context should be captured once and reused everywhere.
* Every generated artifact should become reusable organizational knowledge.
* Planning, execution, and quality engineering should operate as a connected workflow.
* Enterprise governance should be built into the platform from the beginning.

---

# 6. Product Pillars

## Project Intelligence

Understand projects through structured metadata, repository discovery, architecture analysis, and documentation.

## Intelligent Planning

Generate and refine Epics, Features, Stories, Acceptance Criteria, and Tasks using project-specific knowledge.

## Guided Execution

Prepare execution-ready engineering context for developers through Execution Packages, Context Capsules, and repository-aware prompts.

## Quality Engineering

Enable test generation, coverage analysis, validation, and engineering quality gates.

## Enterprise Connectivity

Integrate seamlessly with engineering tools including Azure DevOps, Visual Studio Code, GitHub Copilot, Jira, Confluence, Teams, and future enterprise platforms.

# 9. What Makes HEI Different

HEI does not compete with AI coding assistants.
